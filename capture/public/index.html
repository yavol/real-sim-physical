Capture settings

iPhone: Ultra Wide & landscape

For a wider view, open the Mac’s green Video menu, turn off Centre Stage, hover over its preview, and zoom out to 0.5×. You can also choose Main or Ultra Wide in the Centre Stage options.

For landscape, hold the phone horizontally before enabling its camera. If the picture stays sideways, turn off Portrait Orientation Lock on the iPhone, lock it again, then turn the camera off and on here. The preview and tracking overlay follow the video’s orientation.

Apple’s camera controls ↗

The guide asks you to place each object inside the yellow target area and release it. Two consecutive confident checks confirm each step. Place a box within the area if that's your destination.

The preview can be mirrored. Recording and exported coordinates use the original, unmirrored camera image. Natural narration uses OpenAI TTS with Marin. Talk to guide opens an optional GPT-Live conversation for questions. Microphone audio goes to OpenAI only during that conversation. All guide speech is AI-generated.
